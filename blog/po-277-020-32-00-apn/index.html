Analysis

The Poway 92064 vacant-land market is softening as of the 04/29/2026 effective date. The rural-residential (>=1 acre) cohort that brackets the subject's 9.4-acre site shows a stepped-down median sale price across the 36-, 24-, and 12-month windows ($475,000 - $388,000 - $225,000); median $/acre across the same windows runs $58,000 - $54,545 - $52,966. Closed-sale volume has thinned (25 - 15 - 7 transactions across the same windows), median DOMLS is 154 days (trailing 12 mo all-land), SP/LP ratio is wide (66.0%), and months of inventory at 19.5 places the market firmly in buyer's-market territory. The appraiser has accounted for these conditions in the Sales Comparison Approach through bracketing comparable selection and through period-anchored market-conditions adjustments.
