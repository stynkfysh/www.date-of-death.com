Analysis

In summary, the buyer's market is best characterized as directionally weakening. The anticipated marketing time of 90–120 days reflects current absorption rates. The negative 6-month $/SF trend (-3.4%), paired with a negative 12-month linear $/SF change (-4.0%), indicates that recent activity has softened values from a firmer earlier period. Inventory is soft at 7.8 months, sales volume has pulled back on a quarterly basis, and the SP/LP ratio of 94.0% indicates buyers and sellers are routinely meeting at the list price.
