Date-of-Death Appraisals Throughout Placer County

When a property owner passes away, the estate needs a defensible fair market value as of the date of death. Under IRC §1014, that value becomes the heirs’ new cost basis — the step-up that can eliminate capital gains tax on decades of appreciation. Our desktop appraisal service delivers that valuation for any single-family home or condominium in Placer County, prepared by a California Certified Residential Appraiser and accepted by the IRS, CPAs, and estate attorneys.

Placer County's median home value is approximately $550,000, with typical homes selling in about 33 days. Whether the date of death was last month or years ago, we analyze the comparable sales and market conditions that applied on that specific date.
