Date-of-Death Appraisals Throughout Los Angeles County

When a property owner passes away, the estate needs a defensible fair market value as of the date of death. Under IRC §1014, that value becomes the heirs’ new cost basis — the step-up that can eliminate capital gains tax on decades of appreciation. Our desktop appraisal service delivers that valuation for any single-family home or condominium in Los Angeles County, prepared by a California Certified Residential Appraiser and accepted by the IRS, CPAs, and estate attorneys.

Los Angeles County's median home value is approximately $875,000, with typical homes selling in about 32 days. Whether the date of death was last month or years ago, we analyze the comparable sales and market conditions that applied on that specific date.

Flat-rate pricing statewide: $449 Standard Desktop or $299 Basic Desktop — see pricing details. The entire process is online: submit your order, receive an invoice within one business day, pay, share photos, and download your completed report.

Los Angeles County Property Transfers & the Assessor

Los Angeles County, the largest assessor's office in the nation, reassesses inherited property under Proposition 19 using the date-of-death value reported on the Change in Ownership Statement (PCOR). The county's sheer volume of transfers means processing delays are common — having a completed appraisal ready expedites the reassessment process.
