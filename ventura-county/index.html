Date-of-Death Appraisals Throughout Ventura County

When a property owner passes away, the estate needs a defensible fair market value as of the date of death. Under IRC §1014, that value becomes the heirs’ new cost basis — the step-up that can eliminate capital gains tax on decades of appreciation. Our desktop appraisal service delivers that valuation for any single-family home or condominium in Ventura County, prepared by a California Certified Residential Appraiser and accepted by the IRS, CPAs, and estate attorneys.

Ventura County's median home value is approximately $820,000, with typical homes selling in about 35 days. Whether the date of death was last month or years ago, we analyze the comparable sales and market conditions that applied on that specific date.

Ventura County Property Transfers & the Assessor

Ventura County's mix of agricultural land, coastal communities, and suburban cities creates distinct micro-markets. The county assessor processes Prop 19 claims through the Clerk-Recorder's office, and properties near the coast or in communities like Thousand Oaks and Simi Valley often have significantly different value trajectories than nearby areas.
