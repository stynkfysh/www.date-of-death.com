Statewide California · Certified desktop appraisals, ordered entirely online

For CPAs, Estate Attorneys, Trustees & Fiduciaries

When a client inherits California real estate, the stepped-up basis under IRC §1014 is only as defensible as the documentation behind it. We provide USPAP-compliant retrospective appraisals that establish fair market value as of the date of death — the figure your client will rely on when the property is eventually sold, and the one that must hold up if the IRS asks.

Why Your Client Needs One

  • Establishes the stepped-up basis and eliminates capital gains tax on a lifetime of appreciation
  • Supports the basis claimed on the return when a Form 1099-S reports the eventual sale
  • Documents value for trust accounting, estate settlement, and distribution among beneficiaries
  • Protects against IRC §6662 accuracy-related penalties if the basis is challenged
  • Zestimates, AVMs, and broker price opinions do not meet the qualified-appraisal standard

Scope of Service

  • Property types: Single-family residences and condominiums, including PUDs
  • Purposes: Date of death / step-up in basis, trust valuations, gift tax
  • Coverage: All 58 California counties
  • Effective dates: Retrospective to any date — recent or decades past
  • Not covered: Commercial, 2–4 unit income property, apartment buildings
