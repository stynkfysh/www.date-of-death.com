Date-of-Death Appraisals Throughout San Bernardino County

When a property owner passes away, the estate needs a defensible fair market value as of the date of death. Under IRC §1014, that value becomes the heirs’ new cost basis — the step-up that can eliminate capital gains tax on decades of appreciation. Our desktop appraisal service delivers that valuation for any single-family home or condominium in San Bernardino County, prepared by a California Certified Residential Appraiser and accepted by the IRS, CPAs, and estate attorneys.

San Bernardino County's median home value is approximately $465,000, with typical homes selling in about 42 days. Whether the date of death was last month or years ago, we analyze the comparable sales and market conditions that applied on that specific date.

San Bernardino County Property Transfers & the Assessor

San Bernardino County, the largest county by area in the contiguous United States, includes mountain resort communities, high desert cities, and Inland Empire suburbs with very different market dynamics. The county assessor's office processes a high volume of Prop 13 base-year transfers, making accurate date-of-death valuations essential for property tax outcomes.
